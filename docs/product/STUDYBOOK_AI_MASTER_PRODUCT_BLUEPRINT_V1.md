# StudyBook AI Master Product Blueprint v1

StudyBook AI is an educational application that turns documents into learning experiences with artificial intelligence.

## Product promise

Upload or select a document. Then choose what you want to do with AI.

## Main product areas

1. Inicio: active document, upload/select, AI tools, three recent items.
2. Biblioteca: documents, generated results, AudioBooks and chats.
3. Aprendizaje: continue, simple progress, flashcards, quiz, Voice Tutor and AudioBook activity.
4. Teacher Studio: courses, students, planning, rubrics, question bank, exams, attendance, gradebook and reports.
5. Cuenta: profile, plan, usage, accessibility, language, appearance, privacy and session.

Admin Console exists, but remains hidden and guarded.

## Canonical routes

- `/dashboard`
- `/library`
- `/learning`
- `/teacher`
- `/account`

Historical routes remain compatible as deep links.

## Commercial model

- Free
- Student Pro
- Teacher Pro
- Institution

Accessibility is a capability, not a commercial plan. Ultra is treated as future premium limits/add-ons, not as an independent experience.
