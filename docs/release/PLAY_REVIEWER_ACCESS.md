# Play Reviewer Access - StudyBook AI

Status: `HUMAN ACTION REQUIRED`

StudyBook requires authentication. Reviewer credentials must be entered only
in Play Console App access instructions and the approved password manager. Do
not commit them, place them in screenshots, or include them in support docs.

## Accounts To Provide

1. A confirmed Student account with an active Student entitlement and stable
   sample content owned by that account.
2. A confirmed Teacher account with an active Teacher entitlement and a small
   owned course/unit dataset.
3. No Admin account is required for consumer app review. Student and Teacher
   reviewer accounts must remain denied from Admin.

## Reviewer Journey

- Sign in and open Home.
- Student: Library, document detail, summary/chat, flashcards/quiz, Voice Tutor
  and AudioBook.
- Teacher: Teacher Studio, courses, planning, rubric, question bank and exam.
- Account: confirm current plan and logout.

## Play Console Instructions

- State that login is required and that email confirmation has already been
  completed for the reviewer accounts.
- Provide credentials directly in Play Console, not a private repository URL.
- Describe any deterministic data setup and avoid one-time codes that expire
  before review.
- Verify accounts immediately before rollout and rotate them after review when
  operationally appropriate.
