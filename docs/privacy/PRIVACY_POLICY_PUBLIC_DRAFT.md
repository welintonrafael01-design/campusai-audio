## Approved Content Baseline

- Independent individual accounts require a minimum age of 18 during the
  initial controlled launch. Minor access requires a future guardian or
  authorized-institution consent workflow.
- Account, document, generated study, chat, voice-derived, AudioBook, usage,
  subscription and Teacher data are described according to the technical data
  inventory.
- Users retain the rights they lawfully hold in uploaded content and grant only
  the limited processing permission needed to provide requested services.
- AI output may contain errors and must be reviewed before academic,
  professional or other high-impact reliance.
- Current production providers disclosed are Supabase, OpenAI, Render and
  Vercel. Stripe applies when Web billing is used; Google Play applies only
  when Play billing is active; platform speech applies when voice recognition
  is used.
- Active-account data may be retained as necessary to provide the service.
  After a valid deletion request, active-system deletion or anonymization has a
  maximum operational target of 30 days. Residual backups may remain up to 90
  days. Narrow security, fraud, legal, accounting, tax, dispute or
  defense-of-rights exceptions may be retained for the legitimately required
  period.
- Authenticated deletion removes inventoried owner data and Auth last; partial
  failure remains retryable. External subscriptions require separate provider
  cancellation.
- Requests are received through the approved monitored contact. A message
  alone does not authorize account deletion; reasonable identity verification
  remains required.
- Dominican Republic law and jurisdiction are the approved launch intent.
  Specific venue/court wording remains pending legal consistency review.
- Security language remains factual and does not promise absolute security,
  zero risk or error-free operation.
